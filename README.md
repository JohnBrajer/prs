**Possibility / Risk Perception / Status Payoff**  
A CWI sub-framework by John Brajer.

## Three variables

### Possibility

> Can a human even do this?

Observed examples can move an action from unimaginable to conceivable.

### Risk perception

> What appears to happen to people who try?

Successes, failures, survivorship, visibility, and context all update perceived risk.

### Status payoff

> If I succeed, how extraordinary will society consider it?

As an action becomes common, its differentiation value can decline even while its perceived feasibility increases.

## Dynamic

A new behavior can move through a social field roughly like:

```
low perceived possibility
        |
visible attempt
        v
higher possibility + updated risk
        |
repeated adoption
        v
greater certainty + lower novelty
```

PRS is not a claim that status is the only motive for action. It isolates three variables useful for analyzing how unconventional behavior becomes thinkable, attemptable, and eventually ordinary.
